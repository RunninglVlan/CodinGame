## Algorithms & problem-solving 🧠

These challenges involve more than implementing a known algorithm. My solutions practice:

- **Graph search and traversal** — exploring state spaces, finding paths and reasoning about connectivity
- **Dynamic programming and optimization** — finding efficient solutions when choices and constraints interact
- **Constraint solving and backtracking** — narrowing possibilities while respecting puzzle rules
- **Simulation and state machines** — modeling systems that evolve step by step
- **Geometry and spatial reasoning** — working with coordinates, grids and movement
- **Input parsing and edge cases** — turning strict problem statements into reliable solutions
- **Performance-aware implementation** — meeting runtime limits and refining approaches when straightforward solutions are too slow
